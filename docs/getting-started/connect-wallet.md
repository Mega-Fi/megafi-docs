# Connect Wallet

Connect your Ethereum wallet to MegaFi to start swapping, providing liquidity, and using strategies.

## At a Glance

- Supports major EVM wallets via extension or WalletConnect
- One-click connect from the interface
- Read-only browsing without connecting
- Disconnect anytime

## Connect

1) Visit megafi.app
2) Click "Connect Wallet"
3) Choose your wallet and approve

### WalletConnect (mobile)

1) Select "WalletConnect"
2) Scan the QR in your wallet app
3) Approve the session

## Permissions and Safety

- MegaFi can view your address and request transactions
- Private keys are never exposed
- Review transaction details before approving

## Troubleshooting

- Wallet not detected: ensure extension/app is unlocked, refresh the page
- Multiple wallets: disable unused extensions that may conflict
- Connection rejected: unlock the wallet, retry
- Read-only: connect when prompted to perform actions
