# MegaFi

## Real-Time Options Trading on MegaETH

MegaFi is a DeFi platform built on MegaETH that delivers institutional-grade options trading with sub-10 millisecond execution. Trade options, manage risk, and provide liquidity on the fastest blockchain.

## At a Glance

- **Real-Time Options**: Sub-10ms execution with continuous Greeks updates
- **Complete DeFi Stack**: Options trading (Hedge), DEX, and automated management (CLM)
- **Capital Efficient**: Up to 95% optimized capital utilization
- **Ultra-Low Cost**: Transaction fees under $0.005
- **Built for MegaETH**: 100,000+ TPS with continuous execution

## Why MegaFi

Traditional options platforms are slow and expensive. MegaFi leverages MegaETH's real-time architecture to deliver features impossible on other chains.

**Speed**: Options execute and settle in under 10 milliseconds. Greeks update continuously, not every 12 seconds.

**Efficiency**: Concentrated liquidity and automated strategies achieve 30-50% higher capital utilization than traditional platforms.

**Integration**: Options, DEX, and automated management work together seamlessly.
