MegaFi is a comprehensive DeFi platform built natively on MegaETH, delivering sub-10 millisecond transaction finality and 100,000+ transactions per second. MegaFi combines three core layers - Liquidity, Strategy, and Risk - into a unified interface that redefines what's possible in decentralized finance.

## Why MegaFi

Traditional DeFi platforms are constrained by block times, high fees, and manual management. MegaFi leverages MegaETH's real-time architecture to eliminate these bottlenecks.

**Performance**: Where other platforms measure execution in seconds, MegaFi measures in milliseconds. This enables features impossible on legacy chains - continuous liquidity rebalancing, real-time risk adjustments, and automated trading strategies that execute faster than humanly possible.

**Efficiency**: Concentrated liquidity in MegaPools combined with automated rebalancing achieves 30-50% higher capital utilization than standard liquidity pools. Your capital works harder.

**Simplicity**: Three layers, one interface. Swap tokens, provide liquidity, automate strategies, and hedge risk - all from a single unified platform.
